## **Introduction**

Kingswood University proudly presents _Elements_, a transformative one-year program designed for young adults seeking adventure, personal growth, leadership development, and a deeper understanding of their faith. Through a rich combination of outdoor adventure, community service, academic pursuits, and personal growth opportunities, _Elements_ is crafted for those seeking a foundation for future success rooted in faith, character, and practical skills.

Participants in the _Elements_ program will embark on a unique journey where they gain hands-on experience, certifications, and lifelong friendships within a supportive Christian environment. This holistic program not only nurtures the physical, mental, and spiritual aspects of each participant but also equips them for impactful future careers.

Through this program, students engage in diverse activities that help them grow personally, develop leadership abilities, and strengthen their spiritual journey. By the end of the program, participants will have earned college credits, gained practical certifications, and built a solid foundation for whatever lies ahead.

## **Program Goals**

1. Attract new students to a gap year program
2. Create phenomenal education experiences
3. Integrate Kingswood with Sussex, through service projects and events
4. Help students see opportunities in their city
5. Support local schools
6. Inspire students to receive further education
7. Raise the profile of Kingswood through our unique approach
8. Help students build a solid foundation in faith.

## **Key Components**

### **Outdoor Elements:**

- - **Adventure Experiences:** Engage in activities such as hiking, rock climbing, skiing, kayaking, Multi-day hikes, and survival training. Students will step out of their comfort zones, building resilience and self-confidence.
    - **Leadership Development:** Gain certifications in wilderness first aid, outdoor leadership, and bushcraft skills, providing participants with valuable outdoor safety and leadership training.
    - **Environmental Stewardship:** Learn and practice conservation principles and sustainable living, encouraging a deep respect for the natural world.

### **Discipleship Elements:**

- - **Spiritual Growth:** Strengthen faith through Bible studies, devotionals, and chapel services. Participants will learn to hear God's voice and discern His call for a lifetime of ministry.
    - **Community Building:** Develop meaningful relationships and engage in team-building exercises, fostering a Christ-centered community and a sense of belonging.
    - **Ethical Leadership:** Equip young adults with a strong moral compass, incorporating ethical decision-making and character development into every aspect of the program.

### **Outreach Elements:**

- - **Community Service:** Serve local communities through volunteer work and service initiatives to make a positive impact in our community.
    - **School Partnerships:** Help meet the needs of local schools: tutoring, breakfast programs, TESL, and after-school STEM enrichment.
    - **Social Justice Advocacy:** Participate in projects that address social issues, promoting positive change and demonstrating compassion in action.
    - **Mission Trips:** Experience cross-cultural immersion through mission trips, with a local and international focus, that prepare participants for global ministry work. Learn from local indigenous leaders.
    - **Church Service**: Students serve in a local church all year long. Students may join worship teams, help lead youth groups, or take part in children's ministry all while growing as active participants in local churches.

### **Academic Elements:**

- - **College Credit and Certification:** Upon successful completion, students earn up to 30 credit hours, which are transferable to Kingswood University or other institutions. Certifications include Digital Marketing, wilderness first aid, and field leader Level 1.
    - **Coursework**: Classes include Biblical Studies, Theology, Digital Marketing, Business and Entrepreneurship, and Practical Leadership Development.
    - **Project-Based Learning**: Apply course concepts through hands-on projects in the local community and churches, gaining real-world ministry experience
    - **Interdisciplinary Learning:** Academic subjects such as Business, Leadership, Project Management, and Entrepreneurship are woven into the curriculum, offering participants comprehensive skills applicable to various fields.

### **Leadership Elements**

- - Leadership is woven into all aspects of the program, from backpacking and active ministry involvement to academics and intentional leadership workshops. At Kingswood, we believe that God calls His people to be leaders in this world, no matter where they go or what occupation they decide upon. Leadership looks a little different for everyone. Some leaders stand up front and direct, while others lead in quieter ways behind the scenes, and still others lead simply by following well. The program is designed to teach leadership principles that young leaders can apply in community life, in adventure settings, in front-line ministry, or whatever paths they choose to follow.
    - Students will be given large and small opportunities to lead: small groups, devotionals, hikes, outreach events, and service projects.

## **Program Structure**

The **Elements** program follows a structured calendar that incorporates outdoor adventure, community service, academic coursework, and spiritual growth.

## **Program Benefits**

Participants of the **Elements** program will achieve:

- **Personal Growth**: Develop resilience, confidence, and self-awareness.
- **Leadership Skills**: Enhance leadership abilities through adventure challenges and service work.
- **Career Preparation**: Acquire valuable digital marketing skills and a certification that will aid in future career opportunities.
- **Spiritual Growth**: Build a solid foundation in faith and grow within a supportive Christian community.
- **Lifelong Friendships**: Form meaningful relationships with like-minded peers.
