# Degree in 3

> Source: `Degree in 3 (1).pdf` (two-page concept document). Converted to Markdown for repository reference. The wording and program structure below reflect the source proposal; approval status and current academic requirements should be verified before external use.

## Program Promise

The Kingswood Degree in 3 program provides access to Christian-based degrees at a fraction of the time and cost. Students graduate faster and launch their careers earlier by earning a bachelor's degree in three years.

Instead of earning only a bachelor's degree, students earn three certificates and an associate degree along the way.

## Build a Degree with Certificates

Students earn certificates that stack into associate and bachelor's degrees. Certificates can be completed in less than one year and teach marketable skills that can improve employment options.

### Year 1 Certificate (14-15 credit hours)

Every degree program starts with a Discipleship Training Block focused on experiential learning, community, and discipleship, followed by the first certificate.

A certificate can be completed in one year or less, and every course builds into a degree. The certificate-first approach:

- Allows students to build a customized degree program.
- Provides marketable skills early in the degree process.
- Increases students' chances of completing a bachelor's degree.

### Associate Degree (60 credit hours)

Associate degrees are made up of two certificates plus general and religious education courses. Like the certificates, the associate degree builds into a bachelor's degree.

### Bachelor's Degree (90-96 credit hours)

The proposed focused three-year bachelor's degrees are made up of three certificates plus general courses. The certificates may help students progress in their careers and earn additional income. Courses taken as part of the certificates and associate degree count toward the bachelor's degree.

**Every step = better job opportunities.**

## Degree Example: Bachelor's Degree in Business Management

The bachelor's degree in Business Management gives students analytical, marketing, finance, operations, and leadership skills and prepares them for management positions in a variety of business settings.

To earn the bachelor's degree, students complete three certificates plus additional general education requirements.

| Stage | Credits | Structure and examples |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Certificate | 14-15 | Select two introductory certificates: Introduction to Business, Entrepreneurship, Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Sports Marketing, or Personal Finance. |
| Associate | 60 | Complete the second introductory certificate; Business and Leadership Skills, Business Finance, Organizational Leadership, Project Management, Survey of Accounting, Business Technology, General Education, Bible and Theology, and Ministry. |
| Bachelor's | 90-96 | Complete an advanced certificate; Business Management, Business Ethics, Business Law, Change Management, Human Resource Management, Organizational Health, International Business, and Supervised Experience. |

## Student Value Proposition

### Build a degree with certificates

Students select three certificates that stack into an associate and a bachelor's degree. Certificates can be completed in one year or less and teach marketable job skills that can improve employment prospects sooner.

### Get a job before graduating

The source proposal states that students seeking employment report making more money after completing their first certificate, with a larger income boost after completing a bachelor's degree. This claim requires supporting evidence before publication.

### Customize the degree

Students first choose a bachelor's degree and then select three certificates that build toward it. The proposal positions the certificates, associate degree, and bachelor's degree as being earned within the same overall time and cost as one bachelor's degree.
