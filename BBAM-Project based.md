Accelerated 36-month Bachelor of Arts in Ministry

**Program Overview**

Kingswood University proposes a new **Bachelor of Arts in Ministry**. This program will equip students with the knowledge, skills, and practical experience necessary to serve in Christian ministry, lead within their churches, and make a positive impact in their communities by applying biblical principles and values.

**Program Philosophy**

The **Bachelor of Arts in Ministry** is grounded in the belief that ministry is a calling that combines spiritual leadership with service to others. This program fosters a Christ-centered approach, emphasizing servant leadership, community building, and practical ministry grounded in Scripture.

**Unique Features**

- **Project-Based Learning**: The program shifts away from traditional lectures and emphasizes project-based learning. While completing coursework, students will work on real-world ministry projects with local churches, Christian organizations, and NGOs, gaining hands-on experience and applying their faith in action to meet community needs.
- **Community-Focused Approach**: By partnering with churches and Christian organizations, students will actively serve their communities while developing valuable ministry skills.
- **Interdisciplinary Focus**: The program integrates concepts from various theological and ministry disciplines, as well as business, and technology courses providing students with a comprehensive understanding of practical ministry, theology, leadership, and discipleship.
- **Ministry-Oriented Curriculum**: The **Bachelor of Arts in Ministry** is designed to prepare students for impactful careers in ministry. Graduates will be equipped with the skills and knowledge to lead congregations, serve in Christian organizations, and make disciples.
- **New Ministry Creation**: Following their second year, students will work alone or in teams to create, launch, and lead a ministry initiative, whether it be a church project, community outreach, or nonprofit endeavor.

**Program Objectives**

- To develop students' understanding of biblical principles and their practical application in ministry contexts.
- To equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to lead ministries, preach the Gospel, and serve their communities.
- To foster a strong sense of ethical responsibility, spiritual growth, and servant leadership.
- To prepare students for successful careers in ministry and Christian leadership.

**Curriculum**

The **Bachelor of Arts in Ministry** consists of 120 credit hours over 36 months. The curriculum is divided into eight core modules, each focusing on a specific aspect of Christian ministry. Students will also complete a series of increasingly complex applied projects, working with local churches and ministries to address real-world challenges.

**Program Outcomes**

Upon completion of the **Bachelor of Arts in Ministry**, students will be able to:

- Demonstrate a deep understanding of biblical principles and their application to ministry.
- Lead, inspire, and disciple others to grow in their faith.
- Apply innovative thinking and problem-solving skills to real-world ministry challenges.
- Collaborate effectively with diverse teams within the church and community.
- Preach and teach effectively, communicating the Gospel clearly and persuasively.
- Lead with integrity and humility, embodying Christ-centered servant leadership.
- Develop and launch ministry initiatives that address the needs of churches and communities.

**Portfolio of Projects**

Students will complete projects in four main formats: church-based initiatives, community outreach, new ministry ventures, and missions. Projects will be sourced from churches, Christian organizations, and local communities. Students will build a portfolio of ministry work to demonstrate their skills and readiness to serve in various ministry settings
