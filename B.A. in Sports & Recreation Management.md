## **Course Preview (sports & rec only)**

You asked for just the sports courses—here's your highlight reel:

- **Introduction to Leisure, Sport & Recreation Management  
  **Explore the entire sports & rec landscape—teams, leagues, camps, and community programs.

- **Sports Marketing & Promotion  
  **Sponsorships, endorsements, fan engagement, and how to build a brand fans love.

- **Sports Communication & Media  
  **Content, social, and media relations—tell stories that move fans and protect your org in crisis.

- **Managing Events & Facilities  
  **Plan and run tournaments, games, and community events; manage facilities with excellence.

- **Financial Management in Sports & Recreation (1.5 cr.)  
  **Budgets, ticket revenue, media rights—money matters, explained in sports language.

- **Athletic Administration & Governance ("Playground to Podium")  
  **How policy, compliance, and governing bodies shape sport from rec leagues to elite competition.

- **Sport, Recreation & Society  
  **The big picture: sport's role in culture, character, and global impact—through a biblical lens.

- **Experience Design & Delivery / Event Design & Delivery  
  **Build programs people love—from youth leagues and camps to outdoor adventures.

- **Sports Management Internship (12 cr.)  
  **A supervised field experience with mentoring—graduate with résumé credit, not just credits.
