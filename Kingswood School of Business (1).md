**Kingswood School of Business**

**Empowering Future-Ready Leaders**

At the Kingswood School of Business, we offer programs designed for **every stage of life and career** — from early foundations to advanced leadership. Whether you're exploring business for the first time, accelerating your entrepreneurial journey, or pursuing executive-level influence, our programs are intentionally designed to **connect, build, and grow with you**.

Our programs combine **biblical worldview**, **hands-on application**, and **industry-relevant skills** so graduates are fully equipped to lead in business, ministry, and innovation.

## **Our Program Pathways**

**Step 1:** Foundational Skills → **Certificate Programs** (Entry Point)  
**Step 2:** Launch & Ministry Impact → **Marketplace Ministry Accelerator/Co-vocational Program** (Practical Training)  
**Step 3:** Bachelor-Level Study → **B.A. in Innovation & Sustainability** (3-Year Accelerated Degree)  
**Step 4:** Advanced Leadership → **MOL in Organizational Leadership** (1-Year Management Focus)  
**Step 5:** Executive-Level Mastery → **MBA** (1-Year Fast-Track or 2-Year Full Program)

Each level builds on the previous one, allowing students to **stack credentials**, gain experience, and move seamlessly into higher levels of leadership and influence.

## **5\. Master of Business Administration (MBA) (Executive-Level Program)**

**Duration:** 12, 20, or Flexible Pace _(CB MBA)_

**Review credit requirements**

**Format:** Hybrid — live sessions + self-paced coursework  
**Perfect For:** Business professionals, executives, and mission-driven leaders aiming for organizational transformation.

Our **36-credit MBA** combines **academic rigor**, **practical business challenges**, and **real-world leadership training** through a **Christ-centered lens**. Students gain the strategic, analytical, and leadership skills needed to thrive in today's complex marketplace.

### **Three Flexible Pathways:**

### **1\. One-Year Fast-Track MBA _(Accelerated Option)_**

**Best For:** Students with a **business background** _(BBA/BCom/MMGT or equivalent)_ who want to complete their degree quickly.

- **Duration:** 12 months

- **Structure:** 3 accelerated terms

- **Curriculum:** Reduced core courses + electives

- **Capstone Project:** Summer capstone experience

- **Leadership Summit:** Access to the annual Amplify Leadership Summit _(optional but recommended)  
  _

**2\. Two-Year Full MBA _(Comprehensive Program)_**

**Best For:** Students from **any academic background** seeking a full foundational experience.

- **Duration:** 20 months

- **Structure:** Standard MBA progression across four terms

- **Curriculum:** Comprehensive core curriculum + leadership intensives

- **Electives & Specializations:** Summer electives + deeper tracks when available

- **Capstone Project:** Final term business challenge & leadership integration

### **3\. Competency-Based MBA (CB MBA) _(Coming 2027)_**

**Best For:** Experienced professionals who want **flexibility**, **control**, and the ability to **accelerate** their studies.

- **Duration:** Set your **own pace** within each **16-week term  
  **
- **Learning Model:** Progress by **demonstrating mastery** instead of following a fixed schedule

- **Leverage Prior Experience:** Skip quickly through subjects you've already mastered

- **Focus Where Needed:** Spend more time on topics that are new or challenging

- **Perfect Fit If:  
  **
  - You have **previous work or leadership experience  
    **
  - You prefer **independent learning** over traditional structured timelines

  - You want a **self-paced, flexible MBA** that adapts to your life
