## 4. Strategic Gap Analysis

### Gap 1 - The repo is academically rich but commercially under-documented

There are multiple files supporting academic redesign, experiential learning, Elements and accelerated-degree concepts. There are fewer dedicated documents for campus monetization, accommodation, sponsorship, employer partnerships, executive education, conferences/retreats, facilities, or seasonal use.

**Implication:** The final growth strategy could unintentionally become an academic-program strategy unless the non-tuition portfolio is documented at comparable depth.

### Gap 2 - Summer accommodation should be explicitly captured now

Summer residence rentals and hotel/travel-site listings are strategically important because they use an existing asset during periods of lower student occupancy and can potentially create near-term earned revenue without creating a new degree.

**Current classification:** Exploratory until source evidence, operating feasibility and economics are documented.

**Minimum business case needed:**

1. Available rooms/beds and rentable dates
2. Residence condition and guest expectations
3. Local hotel/short-term-accommodation pricing and summer demand
4. Platform options and commissions
5. Cleaning/turnover and check-in model
6. Insurance, tax, zoning/regulatory and institutional-policy requirements
7. Staffing/security implications
8. Revenue, variable cost and contribution-margin scenarios
9. Mission/reputation guardrails
10. Pilot recommendation

### Gap 3 - Three-year BA is present, but status discipline matters

The repo includes `Degree in 3 (1).pdf`, and the README specifically warns that the three-year degree is **not to be described as approved**. It belongs in the strategy as a proposed pathway requiring academic and regulatory validation, not as a current recruitment promise.

### Gap 4 - Several previously developed initiatives are not obvious in the repo tree

Items that should be located and added if they remain strategically relevant include:

- Upward Sports proposal and Year-1 pro forma
- Amplify Spring 2027 concept/business case
- Festival of Lights written proposal (not only image assets)
- Prior Presidency / Revenue Generation Summary
- Master of Organizational Leadership proposal/current status documentation
- Outdoor Adventure I & II proposal
- School of Business landing-page / program-fit conversion concept
- Summer residence / hospitality revenue concept
- Naming-rights / Business Lab proposal
- Business Accelerator / Tentmaker Ministry concept
- Any financial models supporting recurring-revenue ideas

### Gap 5 - Revenue ideas need economics, not just descriptions

Before prioritization, each material stream should have at least a simple business-case structure:

`Customer -> Offer -> Price -> Volume assumption -> Gross revenue -> Direct costs -> Contribution -> Capacity -> Owner -> Time to launch -> Approval dependencies -> KPI`

This is particularly important because the governing README keeps the historic **$6 million annual recurring-revenue figure by December 2029** as a hypothesis, not an institutional target.

### Gap 6 - Ownership and decision rights remain unresolved

The portfolio crosses Academics, Recruitment/Marketing, Advancement, Student Life, Facilities, Finance, external partnerships and community programming. This reinforces the strategic question behind the proposed **Director of Strategic Growth & Partnerships** function: who owns cross-functional opportunities from validation through launch and measurement?

The role recommendation should continue to emerge from this execution problem rather than from a request to create a position.

## 9. Current Synthesis Conclusion

Kingswood appears to have **more viable growth concepts than it has a system for selecting and executing them**. The emerging strategy is therefore not simply "launch more programs" or "find more revenue." It is to create a coordinated portfolio that connects:

1. **Academic and student pathways** that improve enrolment, differentiation and student outcomes;
2. **Campus, community and partnership activation** that generates recruitment value, mission impact and diversified earned revenue; and
3. **A disciplined growth system** that validates economics, assigns ownership, sequences initiatives, measures results and stops weak ideas early.

The most important immediate synthesis task is now to ensure that the **non-tuition revenue portfolio is as complete and evidence-based as the academic portfolio**, beginning with summer accommodation, campus utilization, events, sponsorship/partnership channels and short-form/executive education.
