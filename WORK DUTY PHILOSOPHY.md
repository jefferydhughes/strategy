**WORK DUTY PHILOSOPHY**

At Kingswood University, we believe in a holistic approach to education. This includes not only academic learning but also practical experience and community engagement. The Elements program is designed to foster a sense of belonging and responsibility within our campus community. Just as in a family, all members share in practical chores, everyone in the KU community is involved in doing work that benefits the total community. As such, all students are expected to contribute to the upkeep and maintenance of our shared spaces through work duties.  

Usually, about two hours per day of the students’ schedule is allotted for work duties.  School staff generally spend many more hours in work per day than that, but their areas of focus are often geared more toward serving the school itself, such as preparing and grading assignments.

It is true that through the student work duties, practical jobs in areas such as the office, kitchen, housekeeping and maintenance get done, so that people don’t have to be hired for these roles and student fees can be kept to a minimum.  But there is a much greater benefit from student work duties:  the formal and informal learning that takes place through staff-student apprenticeship relationships.  This can be a key context for staff (both school and base staff) to encourage student in areas of diligence, stewardship, accountability, etc., but this needs to be done in a context of relationship and not through rules or control.

**The Benefits of Work Duties**

* **Practical Skills Development:** Work duties provide opportunities to develop valuable practical skills, such as time management, problem-solving, and teamwork.  
* **Community Building:** By working alongside fellow students, you'll build lasting friendships and strengthen your sense of community.  
* **Character Development:** Work duties cultivate qualities like responsibility, diligence, and a servant's heart.  
* **Spiritual Growth:** As you serve others, you'll have opportunities to grow spiritually and reflect on your relationship with God.

**The Work Duty Process**

Work duties will be assigned through a fair and equitable process. We encourage you to participate actively in this process, sharing your preferences and skills.

* **Job Selection:** You'll be able to choose from a variety of work duties, such as cleaning, gardening, kitchen assistance, or office work.  
* **Teamwork:** Many work duties are best done in teams, so you'll have the opportunity to collaborate with others and learn from their experiences.  
* **Supervision:** Staff members will be available to provide guidance and support as needed.

**A Shared Responsibility**

Remember, work duties are a shared responsibility. By fulfilling your work duty obligations, you contribute to a positive and productive campus environment. Let's work together to make Kingswood a place where we can learn, grow, and serve.
