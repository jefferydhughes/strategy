## Kingswood University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model

## 1.3 Theoretical Foundations

## Christian Education and Social Constructivism

## Kingswood University's educational model is rooted in Christian principles, guiding students to grow not only academically but also spiritually. While incorporating relevant aspects of contemporary educational theory, such as social constructivism, the university ensures that all learning aligns with its Christian mission of developing Christ-centered leaders for the world

## Social constructivism is the idea that learning occurs in a social context, and knowledge is built through interaction with others and the world. At Kingswood, we believe that true wisdom begins with a foundation in faith. Learning here is not only about intellectual discovery but also about understanding how knowledge is applied within a Christian worldview

## Drawing on thinkers such as John Dewey (1938) and Vygotsky (1978), the social aspect of learning is emphasized. However, we incorporate the belief that the Bible offers ultimate truth and that education should help students interpret their learning through a Christian lens. At Kingswood, students are encouraged to discuss ideas, interpret meaning, and construct knowledge, all while rooted in biblical truth and the teachings of Jesus Christ

## UNESCO's Five Pillars in a Christian Context

## Kingswood University embraces UNESCO's five pillars of education but grounds them in a faith-based approach

## Learning to Know: Building a foundation of knowledge while fostering an understanding of God's creation and His purpose for humanity. Critical thinking is encouraged within the context of faith and Christian ethics

## Learning to Do: Equipping students with practical skills to serve others and make a difference in their communities, professions, and churches, following the example of Christ's servanthood

## Learning to Live Together: Fostering Christian love, respect, and unity in diversity. At Kingswood, students are taught to live out the principles of peace, compassion, and justice, respecting others as they would like to be treated

## Learning to Be: Helping students develop holistically—mind, body, and spirit—toward becoming the people God has called them to be. Creativity, personal discovery, and spiritual growth are central to this pillar

## Learning to Transform Oneself and Society: At Kingswood, transformation comes through Christ. We seek to develop individuals who can lead societal change by modeling biblical principles and working for the common good through acts of service, leadership, and ministry

## Interdisciplinarity and Transdisciplinarity in a Christian Setting

## Kingswood University recognizes the need for interdisciplinarity in addressing complex real-world challenges, while grounding all learning in biblical truth. Our approach integrates multiple fields of study with the Christian mandate to love and serve others. This transdisciplinary approach transcends academic boundaries, fostering collaboration with stakeholders and the broader Christian community to solve the world's problems from a faith-based perspective

## 2.1 Applied and Authentic Learning in Christ

## At Kingswood, education is applied in the real world, always within the framework of Christian service. Learning is not just about acquiring knowledge but also about how that knowledge is used to honor God and serve others. Authentic learning at Kingswood connects classroom knowledge with the realities of ministry, community work, and service in a broken world, with the goal of being Christ's hands and feet

## Interdisciplinary and Transdisciplinary: Students engage in a variety of disciplines, always seeing how each connects to their faith and calling

## Experiential and Participatory: Learning by doing is a key aspect of Kingswood's approach. Whether it's through service-learning projects, internships in ministry, or real-world problem solving, students are constantly encouraged to apply their faith in practical ways

## 2.2 Caring and Community-based Learning

## Kingswood University emphasizes the importance of community, not just as an academic environment but as a spiritual family. Relationships built on Christian values such as love, compassion, and respect are integral to the learning experience

## Inclusive and Diverse: While Kingswood celebrates diversity, the common bond is always faith in Jesus Christ. All students are encouraged to respect one another's backgrounds, with the understanding that true unity is found in Christ

## Team-based and Co-creative: Collaboration is encouraged, reflecting the biblical principle of working together as the body of Christ. Students learn in teams, serving together and supporting each other, just as we are called to serve within the Christian community

## 2.3 Transformational Learning through Christ

## Kingswood University is committed to transformational learning, both academically and spiritually. We believe that education is a means for individuals to be transformed by Christ, leading to the transformation of society through servant leadership

## Social Innovation and Christian Leadership: Students at Kingswood are taught to be changemakers, but change is understood as transformation through Christ. Whether in ministry, business, education, or other fields, students are trained to lead with integrity, humility, and compassion, guided by their Christian faith

## Respect for Indigenous Peoples and Traditions: Kingswood respects all cultures and traditions, understanding that each person is made in the image of God. We encourage students to engage respectfully and lovingly with all people, fostering reconciliation and healing in line with biblical justice and mercy

## 2.4 Reflective and Impactful Learning

## Kingswood University fosters reflective practice, encouraging students to continually assess how their learning aligns with their faith and how they can apply it to make a positive impact on the world. Reflection is seen as an act of spiritual discipline, allowing students to grow closer to God as they pursue knowledge

## Impactful: Students are equipped to go into the world and make a lasting, positive impact, whether that's in their local church, community, or broader society. Every learning experience is designed to help students fulfill their God-given calling

## Conclusion: Learning for a Life of Christ-Centered Leadership

## At Kingswood University, we believe that education is about more than academic success. It's about transformation—of the self, of society, and of the world—through the power of Christ. Our learning model is designed to produce leaders who live out their faith, serve others, and lead in ways that reflect the love and truth of Jesus Christ

**Proposal: Adopting the Royal Roads University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model at Kingswood University**

**Executive Summary**

This proposal outlines the potential benefits of adopting the Royal Roads University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model (LTRM) at Kingswood University. By implementing the LTRM, Kingswood can foster a more engaging, collaborative, and transformative learning environment for its students. The LTRM's emphasis on applied learning, community-based engagement, and interdisciplinary collaboration aligns with Kingswood's mission and values.

**Introduction**

Kingswood University is committed to providing a high-quality education that prepares students for success in a rapidly changing world. To achieve this goal, the university must continually evolve its teaching and learning practices. The Royal Roads University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model (LTRM) offers a promising framework for guiding Kingswood's educational initiatives.

**The Royal Roads University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model**

The LTRM is a comprehensive approach to education that emphasizes the following core principles:

- **Applied and Authentic Learning:** Students should engage in real-world experiences and apply their knowledge to solve practical problems.
- **Caring and Community-Based Learning:** A supportive and inclusive learning environment is essential for student success.
- **Interdisciplinary and Transdisciplinary Learning:** Students should be able to connect knowledge from multiple disciplines to address complex issues.

**Benefits of Adopting the LTRM at Kingswood University**

Adopting the LTRM at Kingswood University can offer several significant benefits, including:

- **Enhanced Student Engagement:** The LTRM's emphasis on applied and authentic learning can increase student motivation and interest in their studies.
- **Improved Learning Outcomes:** By providing students with opportunities to apply their knowledge in real-world contexts, Kingswood can help them develop deeper understanding and critical thinking skills.
- **Stronger Community Connections:** The LTRM's focus on community-based learning can foster partnerships with local organizations and provide students with opportunities to contribute to their communities.
- **Enhanced Faculty Professional Development:** Implementing the LTRM can support faculty in developing innovative teaching methods and staying up-to-date with the latest pedagogical approaches.

**Implementation Plan**

To successfully implement the LTRM at Kingswood University, the following steps should be taken:

1. **Faculty Training and Development:** Provide faculty with training on the LTRM principles and best practices.
2. **Curriculum Review and Revision:** Evaluate existing curricula and make necessary adjustments to align with the LTRM.
3. **Development of New Programs:** Create new programs that incorporate the LTRM principles, such as experiential learning courses and community-based research projects.
4. **Assessment and Evaluation:** Develop a system for assessing the effectiveness of the LTRM implementation and making necessary adjustments.

**Conclusion**

The Royal Roads University Learning, Teaching, and Research Model offers a valuable framework for improving the quality of education at Kingswood University. By adopting the LTRM, Kingswood can create a more engaging, collaborative, and transformative learning environment for its students.
