## Outcomes

Outcome Descriptions: A Blueprint for Success

Outcome descriptions outline the specific skills, knowledge, and character traits you'll develop upon successfully completing the Kingswood Elements Program. These statements serve as a guide for your mentor team, who will assess your competence in each outcome.

By successfully completing all 10 outcomes, you'll earn a 1-year, 30-credit hour Certificate in Christian Foundations, demonstrating your mastery of the essential elements of Christian faith and practice.

## Measuring Outcomes

The Kingswood Elements Program consists of 10 outcomes, each worth 3 credit hours. While these outcomes are similar to traditional 3-credit courses, they are distinct in their approach. Instead of focusing solely on specific topics and assignments, outcomes emphasize a holistic and integrated learning experience.

The learning context for Elements is real life, and the goal is to demonstrate competence in a collection of related competencies that may span across multiple topics. These competencies address not only knowledge and skill but also character and behavior.

As Christians, our way of being—our character and behavior—is often more impactful than our knowledge or ability to articulate our faith. The Elements Program is designed to equip you with the knowledge, skills, and character necessary to effectively live out your faith in your daily life.
