# Kingswood University Year-Round Campus Activation Strategy

**Prepared:** September 9, 2026  
**Working status:** Proposed strategic platform  
**Purpose:** Capture the idea that Kingswood should create year-round reasons for students, families, churches, schools, community organizations, entrepreneurs, ministry leaders, tourists, and other visitors to come onto campus.

---

## Strategic Thesis

Kingswood's campus should function as a **year-round platform for education, ministry, community engagement, recruitment, partnerships, and appropriate earned revenue** rather than as an asset used primarily during the traditional academic calendar.

The goal is not simply to host more events. It is to build a deliberate annual rhythm of campus activity that:

1. Brings new people onto campus;
2. Deepens relationships with churches, schools, families, employers, and community organizations;
3. Creates natural recruitment and program-discovery opportunities;
4. Generates non-tuition revenue where appropriate;
5. Uses residences, classrooms, chapel, recreation spaces, grounds, food service, and future facilities more productively;
6. Strengthens Kingswood's identity as a Christian university serving its region and wider church community.

## Core Principle: Every Season Should Have a Reason to Visit Kingswood

### Fall

Potential campus activators include:

- Leadership and professional-development events
- Church and ministry gatherings
- Entrepreneur / business events
- MBA and executive-education intensives
- Youth and school events
- Community partnerships
- Athletic and recreation programming
- Chapel and auditorium use by partner organizations

### Winter / Christmas

Potential campus activators include:

- **Festival of Lights / Christmas campus experience**
- Carol sing and Christmas programming
- Elementary-school Christmas concerts in the chapel
- Church Christmas events
- Seasonal community gatherings
- Winter recreation and family events where feasible
- Conferences and retreats during available periods

The objective is for Kingswood to become a recognized regional Christmas destination while creating repeated community exposure to the campus.

### Spring

Potential campus activators include:

- **Amplify Leadership Summit**
- Conferences for entrepreneurs and business leaders
- Conferences / intensives for co-vocational and marketplace ministers
- Church leadership gatherings
- Executive education
- School and community events
- High-school graduations hosted in the chapel or other suitable facilities
- Youth sports, tournaments, and recreation programming

### Summer

Potential campus activators include:

- **Summer camps**
- Youth and ministry camps
- Sports camps
- Leadership / entrepreneurship camps
- Outdoor adventure programming
- Church retreats and conferences
- Entrepreneur / MBA intensives
- **Summer residence rentals**
- Conference housing
- Public accommodation listed through Kingswood's website and appropriate booking platforms
- Weddings, reunions, retreats, and facility rentals where mission/policy permits
- High-school graduation ceremonies and other community rentals

Summer should be treated as a deliberate asset-utilization season rather than simply an academic off-season.

## Revenue Model

Not every campus activity needs to maximize direct profit. Each initiative should be evaluated on a blended return:

### Direct Revenue

- Facility rental
- Registration fees
- Tuition / executive-education fees
- Camp fees
- Accommodation revenue
- Food-service revenue
- Sponsorship
- Recreation memberships and court bookings

### Recruitment Value

- Prospective-student visits
- Graduate-program leads
- Church referrals
- Family exposure
- Email/contact capture
- Campus tours and program information

### Partnership Value

- Church relationships
- School relationships
- Employer relationships
- Municipal/community goodwill
- Sponsorship and donor cultivation

### Mission Value

- Christian community service
- Ministry development
- Leadership development
- Youth engagement
- Community hospitality

An initiative may therefore be worthwhile even if direct event margin is modest, provided its recruitment, partnership, or mission return is intentional and measurable.

The current working portfolio includes:

1. **Spring:** Amplify Leadership Summit
2. **Summer:** Camps, retreats, conferences, and public/conference accommodation
3. **Fall:** Business, leadership, church, and executive-education programming

---

## Key Strategic Guardrail

**More visitors is not the strategy by itself.**

Every recurring event or rental should have an explicit primary job:

- Generate revenue;
- Recruit students;
- Build a strategic partnership;
- Serve the community / mission;
- Or deliberately accomplish a combination of these.

Events that consume substantial staff time without a measurable strategic return should be redesigned or retired.
