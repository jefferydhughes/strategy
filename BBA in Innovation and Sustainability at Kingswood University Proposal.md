#

Are you ready to be part of a movement that is transforming the business world? Our BBA in Innovation and Sustainability at Kingswood University offers a unique opportunity to channel your passion for a better planet into a rewarding career. As a future business leader, you'll learn to balance profitability with purpose, driving innovation and creating sustainable solutions that benefit both society and the environment.

## Program Overview

Kingswood University proposes a new Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Innovation and Sustainability. This program will equip students with the knowledge, skills, and practical experience necessary to drive positive change in the business world by fostering innovative and sustainable solutions.

## Program Philosophy

The BBA in Innovation and Sustainability is grounded in the belief that businesses can and should operate in a way that benefits both the environment and society. This program fosters a "capitalism with a conscience" approach, emphasizing ethical, responsible, and sustainable business practices.

## Unique Features

- **Project-Based Learning:** The program shifts away from traditional courses and emphasizes project-based learning. While completing coursework, students will work on real-world projects with community partners, local businesses, and NGOs, gaining hands-on experience and applying their knowledge to address pressing issues.
- **Community-Focused Approach:** By partnering with local organizations, students will contribute to the well-being of their community while developing valuable skills.
- **Interdisciplinary Focus:** The program integrates concepts from various business disciplines, providing students with a comprehensive understanding of sustainable business practices.
- **Career-Oriented Curriculum:** The BBA in Innovation and Sustainability is designed to prepare students for successful careers in the field of sustainable business. Graduates will be equipped with the skills and knowledge to lead change in organizations of all sizes.
- **New Venture Creation:** Students will work alone, or in teams to create, launch, and run a sustainable business venture following their second year.

## Program Objectives

- To develop students' understanding of sustainable business practices and their impact on society and the environment.
- To equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to drive innovation and create sustainable solutions.
- To foster a strong sense of ethical responsibility and social consciousness among students.
- To prepare students for successful careers in the field of sustainable business.

## Curriculum

The BBA in Innovation and Sustainability consists of 120 credit hours over 36 months. The curriculum will be divided into eight core modules, each focusing on a specific aspect of sustainable business. Students will also complete a series of increasingly complex applied projects, working with community partners to address real-world challenges.

**Integrated Marketing Communications**

This IMC course provides key instruction on the concepts and methods in marketing communications for traditional and digital media, including a foundation in the development and execution of integrated communications strategies for an organization. Primary emphasis is placed on consumer insight, branding, market segmentation and positioning, message strategy, promotion, and the execution of marketing communications practice. Instruction is to be accomplished through lectures, discussions, quizzes, and the development of an IMC campaign for an organization.

**Entrepreneurship**

Entrepreneurial Leadership is designed to help students learn three unique approaches to business growth: entrepreneurship, intrapreneurship, and non-profit creation. Topics include business plan development, market analysis, business models, funding, intellectual property, sales, marketing, and personnel.

**Advertising**

Students will build their foundation in the advertising industry through real-world projects, industry-related assignments, and relative case studies featuring real businesses. This course covers topics like strategy development, legal and ethical challenges, Branding, Evaluating effectiveness, and much more.

**Market Research**

Understanding how to properly conduct market research is a fundamental part of any marketing strategy. In this course, students will learn the process of conducting market research from start to finish, including how to effectively present and communicate the results. With the need for professionals in market research increasing each year, students will be prepared for the industry as they go through this course.

**Digital Marketing**

This class gives students a theoretical understanding of the internet marketplace that is necessary to adapt to its many changes, while also equipping you with the skills you'll need to perform vital daily functions. Students will gain experience in search engine optimization, website analytics, pay-per-click advertising, AI, and social media marketing. Explores how to package learnings in a documented plan for focused and influential storytelling

**Personal Branding**

This course will explore many aspects of personal branding, including identifying various perspectives of personal branding, creating your brand, formulating brand partnerships, handling challenges, ethical considerations, and strategic planning.

Content includes strategic elements, content creation, writing, and digital and social media elements to help you build a successful personal brand.

**New Venture Creation**

This course teaches students methods for generating ideas for new ventures, the process of determining the market, and how to estimate if the market is big enough to achieve your goals. For a large portion of the course, students will be working to launch an actual product that they come up with. The focus will be on sustainable, and, or, local products.

**International Marketing**

Through this course, students will partner with an international organization to help them develop and market their organization and unique products. They will study local marketing trends, create a marketing plan, and launch a new product on social media campaigns.

**Digital Marketing and E-Commerce**

This class will give students an understanding of the internet marketplace that is necessary to adapt to its many changes, while also equipping students with the skills to perform marketing and e-commerce roles. By the end of the course students will be able to build websites, digital products, sales funnels, and marketing materials.

**Social Innovation and Social Finance**

Introduces strategies employed by enterprises that wish to incorporate social benefits and positive change into their organization. Explores the role of finance in promoting social innovation and contributing toward solutions for a range of contemporary issues. Includes methods for measuring and managing social impact. Identifies strategies for scaling up social innovations for broader impact.

**Project Managemen**t

Provides a foundation for effective project management that delivers on an end goal in alignment with business strategies and goals. This course provides an in-depth understanding of methodologies, applications, and skills needed to be a successful project manager.

**Financial Management**

Designed to help students develop the skills and knowledge required to manage the financial health of an organization. The course is comprised of three main sections: (1) financial monitoring, measuring, and reporting; (2) using financial data to make managerial decisions to improve organizational performance; and (3) financing and investing. Specific attention is given to projects related to financial management for innovation and sustainability.

**Strategic Management & Planning**

Strategic Management is the art and science of enabling organizations to achieve their strategic objectives. This course equips students with the tools to analyze environments, formulate strategies, and implement plans effectively. Key topics include understanding organizational environments, designing strategic actions, developing actionable plans, and executing strategies to achieve success.

**Ecology and Management**

Students will study a business within the Fundy Region, a socio-ecological system in which societal (human) and ecological (biophysical) subsystems are linked through mutual dependencies and interaction.

They will examine the socio-ecological implications of various economic institutions, business models, products/services and processes, with particular emphasis on those that produce beneficial ecological outcomes. Explores business and management within the context of socio-ecological causes and consequences of climate change, biodiversity loss, pollution and toxification, and other critical environmental issues of the 21st century.

**Leading with Systems**

Students will explore the complex characteristics of dynamic systems to develop an understanding of the interrelatedness of the systems. Specific emphasis on systems for leading and managing teams and understanding how goals, motivations, incentives, structures, and accountability influence individual, team, and organizational dynamics.

## Program Outcomes

Upon completion of the BBA in Innovation and Sustainability, students will be able to:

- Demonstrate a deep understanding of sustainable business practices and their impact on society and the environment.
- Apply innovative thinking and problem-solving skills to address complex business challenges.
- Collaborate effectively with diverse teams to achieve shared goals.
- Communicate effectively and persuasively, both verbally and in writing.
- Lead and inspire others to embrace sustainable business practices.
- Have the innovation, and sustainability-focused experiences that global leaders are calling for
- Tackle the social and environmental issues they care about.

## Portfolio of Projects

Projects will take one of four styles: client-base, simulation, new ventures, or challenges. The will be sourced from organizations in the private, public, and non-profit sectors. After completing the wide range of projects, students will have built a portfolio of work that will directly demonstrate the skills acquired to their potential employers.

**Business Administration Applied Project 1 and Simulated Projects 1, 2** (**6 credits)**

As a foundation for subsequent project work in the program, students are oriented to project management, including project planning, engaging and working with clients and stakeholders, team building and teamwork, creative problem solving, business fundamentals, and effective communication strategies. In teams, students work on their first set of projects, which may take the form of challenges, simulations, and client-driven business or social initiatives.

**Business Administration Applied Project 2 (6 credits)**

Students extend their knowledge and practice in business and sustainability through ongoing study in specific subject areas. Learning is directly applied to more extensive projects with community clients. Students develop and practice their skills in projects that integrate subject-specific topics and broaden their portfolio of work experience.

**Business Administration Applied Project 3 (6 credits)**

In a third set of projects, students continue to extend their knowledge and deepen their practice in business and sustainability through ongoing study in specific subject areas. Learning is directly applied to projects with community clients that are each incrementally more complex.

**Business Administration Applied Project 4 (15 credits)**

Students gain practical experience by launching their own businesses through the summer.

Students will create a pitch and apply for financing.

Upon acceptance of their proposal, students will be offered an interest-free loan to create summer employment and gain valuable business experience by operating their own business. The Student Entrepreneurship component provides a guaranteed interest-free loan of up to \$3,000 to help students start a summer business that will create at least one full-time student summer job for themselves (minimum of 30 hours per week) and provide the applicant with valuable entrepreneurial experience. If the loan has been repaid in full by October 15th, the entire amount will be applied to their tuition, provided that all program regulations and requirements have been met.

## Degree Completion Options 6 Credits

We offer three options for students to complete their Bachelor of Business Administration.

Students can choose the option that works best for their goals.

In all completion options, students will create a portfolio of work that documents and showcases their learning and skill development.

### Option 1: Capstone Project

Students pursue a research project within an area of interest related to business and sustainability. The project must be comprehensive in scope and provide the student with the opportunity to demonstrate their mastery of the core competencies developed in the program. All projects incorporate input from industry/sector stakeholders and identify opportunities for innovation and/or change to achieve a public or social good (i.e., beyond personal profit).

### Option 2: Internship

Provides students with opportunities to apply their skills and to integrate concepts from their previous coursework in the context of a professional setting. In this course, students complete an internship (a minimum of 12 weeks at the workplace) and submit reflective assignments demonstrating how the internship integrates with the core concepts of the program.

Students can work at local businesses or international non-profits

Prerequisites Successful completion of all applied projects.

Successful completion of an internship preparation course.

### Option 3: International Semester

Students can study abroad while continuing project-based learning in business, innovation, and sustainability. Students will complete at least six credits at one of our partner universities.
