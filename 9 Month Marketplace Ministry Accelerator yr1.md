**Goal:** Equip ministry professionals with the tools and support to launch and operate successful businesses, achieving financial self-sufficiency.

**Schedule:**

- **In Person Kick-off event**
- **Weekly Meetings (1 hour):**
  - Interactive sessions covering key business areas.
  - Guest speakers from relevant industries.
  - Peer-to-peer discussions and problem-solving.
- **Monthly Mastermind Sessions (2 hours):**
  - Focused discussions and accountability check-ins.
  - Peer coaching and brainstorming opportunities.
  - Guest mentors provide personalized guidance.
- **In Person Conference & Pitch**

**Program Outline:**

**Month 1: Self-Discovery & Business Idea Launch**

- **Meetings:**
  - Self-assessment: Identifying strengths, interests, and skills.
  - Market validation: Brainstorming business ideas and assessing market needs.
  - Lean Canvas Introduction: Building a one-page business model framework.
- **Homework:**
  - Develop a personal mission statement.
  - Research potential business ideas & conduct initial market research surveys.
  - Start crafting a Lean Canvas for your top business idea.

**Month 2: Customer Validation & Business Model Development**

- **Meetings:**
  - Customer interviews: Refine your target market & validate customer needs.
  - Develop your value proposition: How will you solve your customer's problem?
  - Competitive analysis: Identify key competitors and your unique selling proposition (USP).
- **Homework:**
  - Conduct in-depth customer interviews and analyze results.
  - Define your value proposition and USP.
  - Refine your Lean Canvas with customer insights.

**Month 3: Financial Planning & Business Operations**

- **Meetings:**
  - Financial forecasting: Develop a basic financial model for your business.
  - Legal structures & business registration: Sole proprietorship, LLC, etc.
  - Business operations: Setting up systems for accounting, marketing, and customer management.
- **Homework:**
  - Create a financial plan with revenue projections, cost estimates, and break-even analysis.
  - Research and choose a business structure.
  - Start building basic systems for accounting, marketing, and customer management.

**Month 4: Marketing & Sales Strategies**

- **Meetings:**
  - Marketing fundamentals:.
  - Sales strategies: Developing sales funnels and building a customer acquisition plan.
- **Homework:**
  - Develop your brand identity and design marketing materials.
  - Create a customer acquisition plan with specific tactics for each marketing channel.

**Month 5 & 6: Product/Service Development & Implementation**

- **Meetings:**
  - Product/Service Development: Creating a minimum viable product (MVP) based on customer feedback.
  - Guest speakers: Experts in product development, branding, and legal compliance.
  - Develop systems for customer service and fulfillment
- **Homework:**
  - Create a prototype or MVP of your product/service.
  - Refine your marketing materials based on your MVP.
  - Implement systems for customer service and product fulfillment.

**Month 7 & 8: Launch & Growth**

- **Meetings:**
  - Soft launch and initial marketing campaign execution.
  - Analyze customer data and feedback for growth strategies.
  - Funding options for business expansion (loans, grants, etc.).
- **Homework:**
  - Launch your business with a targeted marketing campaign.
  - Track key metrics such as website traffic, conversion rates, and customer feedback.
  - Research and develop a strategy for securing funding for future growth.

**Month 9: Building Sustainability & Beyond**

- **Meetings:**
  - Building a sustainable business model for long-term success.
  - Time management strategies for managing a business alongside a full-time role.
  - Alumni panel discussion with successful program graduates.
- **Homework:**
  - Develop strategies for work-life balance and managing both your business and non-profit role.
  - Create a long-term business plan outlining your vision for the future of your company.

**Additional Resources:**

- Online platform with course materials, templates, and industry resources.
- Access to one-on-one consultations with business advisors (optional).
- Networking opportunities with other program participants and alumni.
